This website records limited technical information needed to understand site
traffic and prevent abuse. A visit may include the time, requested page,
referring page, browser user agent, language, screen size, approximate location
provided by the hosting network, a truncated network prefix, and a keyed hash
derived from the IP address. Complete IP addresses are not retained by default.

These records are used only for aggregate traffic analysis and security. They
are not sold or used for advertising, and are automatically deleted after 30
days. Browsers that enable Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track do not send
the site's visit beacon.

For questions or deletion requests, please use the contact information on the
homepage.

本站会记录用于流量分析及防止滥用的有限技术信息，可能包括访问时间、访问页面、来源页面、
浏览器标识、语言、屏幕尺寸、托管网络提供的大致位置、截断后的网络前缀，以及由 IP 地址生成
的带密钥哈希值。默认情况下不保留完整 IP 地址。

这些记录仅用于汇总流量分析和安全保障，不会出售或用于广告，并会在 30 天后自动删除。
启用 Global Privacy Control 或 Do Not Track 的浏览器不会发送本站的访问记录请求。

如需咨询或请求删除数据，请通过主页上的联系方式与我联系。
