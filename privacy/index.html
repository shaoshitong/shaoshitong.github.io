Privacy

This website records limited technical information needed to understand site traffic and prevent abuse. A visit may include the time, requested page, referring page, browser user agent, language, screen size, approximate location provided by the hosting network, a truncated network prefix, and a keyed hash derived from the IP address. The complete IP address is also retained for the same limited purposes.

These records are used only for aggregate traffic analysis and security. They are not sold or used for advertising, and are automatically deleted after 30 days. Browsers that enable Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track do not send the site’s visit beacon.

For questions or deletion requests, please use the contact information on the homepage.
